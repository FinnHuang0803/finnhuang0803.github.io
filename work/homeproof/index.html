Homeproof

Every photo you take while renting protects your deposit.

Role
Product Designer
Year
2026
Type
iOS app (concept)

Deposit disputes come down to one question: was it like this before? Renters rarely have the proof.

Homeproof turns move-in, meter readings, and repairs into one timeline of evidence both sides can trust.

One timeline turns every renting moment into evidence.

  1. 01

    AI flags, people decide.

    AI points out differences. Renter and landlord confirm every call.

  2. 02

    Fair to both sides.

    Landlords confirm through a link: no app, no login, the same locked copy.

  3. 03

    Every photo pays off now.

    Each capture returns an answer right away: a bill, a status, a score.

Guided capture makes good evidence the easy path.

Thirty seconds a month keeps the record alive.

Same evidence, fewer arguments.

At move-out, Homeproof lays the move-in photo over the camera as a faint ghost. Line the two up and the shutter fires on its own, so every spot is shot from the same angle as on move-in. The results then sort themselves into what is new and what was already there.
