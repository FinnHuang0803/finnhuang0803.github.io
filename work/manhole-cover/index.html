Recycled Rubber Manhole Cover

A safer, greener manhole cover made from recycled rubber.

Role
Industrial Designer
Year
2025
Type
Industrial design

Red Dot Award: Design Concept 2025, Best of the Best

It combines recycled rubber with a reusable metal base. A recycled-rubber pad on top adds grip and wear resistance, making roads safer for everyone who crosses them while cutting maintenance costs and environmental impact. When the pad wears out, it is swapped and recycled while the metal frame stays in place.
