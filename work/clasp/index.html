Clasp

A crypto wallet that works like a real one.

Role
Product Designer
Year
2026
Type
iOS app (concept)

Most crypto wallets show money as a list of tokens. Clasp keeps it in pockets you can see, pull out and move by hand, so every transfer feels deliberate.

Moving money is a physical act.

See the worst case before you swap.

Concept project for portfolio purposes. Clasp is a fictional product. Balances and transactions are fictional.
